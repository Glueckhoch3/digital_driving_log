Fuel Tracking

Fuel Refill Management

Track fuel refills for accurate fuel consumption and cost calculations. Fuel refills are associated with specific drives and help monitor fuel efficiency.

Note: Fuel refills can be added through the Driving Log page by selecting a drive and recording the refill details.

Features

  • Record fuel refill date, amount, and cost
  • Automatic cost per liter calculation
  • Link refills to specific drives
  • Track fuel consumption over time
  • Monitor fuel cost trends
